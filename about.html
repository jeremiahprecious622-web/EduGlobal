About EduGlobal

Learn. Grow. Achieve.

EduGlobal is an online learning platform for students from Nigeria and around the world. Your future has no borders.

Our mission

To make good teaching reachable on a phone. Students should be able to join a live class, revise from a recorded lesson, practise for an exam and ask for help, all in one place.

We start with the Nigerian curriculum and are adding other countries and examination systems over time.

What you get

  • Live classes through Google Meet
  • Recorded lessons to watch any time
  • Practice exams with explanations
  • An AI study assistant for revision help
  • WhatsApp classrooms to stay in touch

What we stand for

Trust

Teachers are reviewed by an administrator before they teach.

Access

Built to work on affordable Android phones and slow connections.

Safety

We collect only the information we need and take care with young learners.

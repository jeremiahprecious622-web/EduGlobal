Privacy Policy

How we handle your information. We collect only what we need to run your classes.

Who we are

EduGlobal is an online learning platform. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, please use the Contact page.

What we collect from students

  • Full name, email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase and we cannot read it)
  • Year of birth (not the full date), used to check the minimum age of 10 and to ask for parent or guardian agreement for learners under 13
  • Country, class, chosen subjects, time zone and language
  • Messages you write in class chat, and your progress on the platform

What we collect from teacher applicants

  • Name, email, phone number, country and qualifications
  • Your CV and qualification documents, which only the administrator can open
  • Your answers, demo lesson link and a referee contact

Why we use it

  • To create your account, place you in the right class and show you the right lessons
  • To send verification and password reset emails
  • To keep the classes safe, including removing messages or accounts that break the rules
  • To review teacher applications

Children and young people

EduGlobal is for learners aged 10 and above. Learners under 13 must have a parent or guardian who agrees to their joining. In class chat, we show only a first name and the first letter of the last name. We do not ask for home addresses or full dates of birth.

Who else handles your information

We use Google Firebase to store accounts and data, Google Meet for live classes, YouTube for lesson videos and WhatsApp for optional class groups. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data using the Contact page.

Keeping information safe

Access is restricted by role. Students only see their own class, teachers only see their own classes, and teacher documents can only be opened by the administrator. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. For learners under 13, a parent or guardian can make these requests.

Terms of Use

Simple rules that keep EduGlobal useful and safe for everyone.

Using EduGlobal

By creating an account you agree to these terms. EduGlobal is for learners aged 10 and above. Learners under 13 need a parent or guardian to agree. Teachers must be 18 or older.

Your account

  • Give true information and keep your password private
  • Choose your class honestly. Only the administrator can change it
  • One person, one account. Do not share your login

Behaviour in classes and chat

  • Be kind and respectful to teachers and classmates
  • Keep chat about learning. No bullying, hate, spam, adult content or sharing of personal details such as addresses or phone numbers
  • Do not share live class links with people outside your class
  • Teachers and the administrator may remove messages, suspend accounts or end access for anyone who breaks these rules

Live classes and lessons

Live classes run on a timetable and may change. They use Google Meet. Lesson content is for your own learning and may not be copied or sold.

Teachers

Applying does not guarantee a place. The administrator reviews each application and decides who is admitted. A teacher may be suspended or removed at any time for breaking these terms.

No guarantee of exam results

We work hard to help you learn, but we cannot promise any exam result.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms. If you have questions, please use the Contact page.
